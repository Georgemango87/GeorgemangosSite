Training examples tell the network what answer it should produce for a given set of inputs. During training, the network adjusts its weights and biases to reduce the difference between its prediction and the expected output.

How This Works

• Neuron: Multiplies incoming signals by weights, adds bias, and passes through an activation function.
• Forward Pass: Propagates data from inputs to outputs.
• Backprop: Adjusts weights to minimize prediction error.
• Large layers: Layers with more than 24 neurons are simulated in full, but only 24 are drawn per layer to keep the canvas readable.
• Fast training: The training loop caches the graph topology and uses typed arrays for the math. Each frame trains for ~20ms, then yields to keep the UI responsive.
• Shortcuts: Shift+Click a spawner card to quick-build · Mouse wheel zooms · Press F to fit. On touch: tap a spawner card, pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
