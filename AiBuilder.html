Build Your Own AI

Teach it what you want it to know. No coding or giant datasets required.

You teach your AI concepts, facts, and behaviors. It learns patterns from your examples and uses them to respond to new messages.

1 · Teach concepts

Give your AI a topic and a few example sentences. It works out the important words on its own — you never write a keyword list.

2 · Add facts & rules

Tell it your name, your favorite color, or how it should greet people. It remembers and reuses them.

3 · Chat & correct

Talk to it. When it gets something wrong, click “Teach this” and it learns immediately.

4 · See its brain

A simple map of what it knows, plus an explanation of exactly why it gave each answer.

An honest note: your AI is not a giant language model and does not understand language like a person. It is a small pattern-matching system that runs entirely in your browser. It recognises patterns in the examples you teach it, remembers facts you give it, and follows the rules you set. That is the whole trick — and it is genuinely yours.
