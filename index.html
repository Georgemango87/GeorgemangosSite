About

How I work.

I'm not a classically trained programmer. I build by defining a problem clearly, producing a working first version with AI-assisted tools, and then refining it until it behaves the way I intended. I write some of the code myself — layout and CSS, small logic fixes, the occasional function — but the majority of these projects comes from directing those tools and editing their output.

What I contribute is the concept, the judgement and the persistence. If a tool takes more than a click to open, or is confusing for even a moment, I treat it as unfinished and keep going until it isn't.

I'd rather be straightforward about the process: these projects are not typed from scratch. They are directed, curated and refined. The ideas are mine; the implementation is a collaboration.
