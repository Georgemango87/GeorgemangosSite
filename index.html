About

I vibe code. Here's what that actually means.

I'm not a classically trained programmer. I build by describing what I want, generating it, and then steering it until it works the way I pictured. I write a bit of code myself — small fixes, CSS, layout tweaks, the occasional function — but the bulk of these projects comes from working with AI tools and editing the output.

What I bring is the idea, the taste, and the persistence to keep pushing until it's fun to use. If a tool takes more than a click to open, or feels confusing for two seconds, it's not done yet.

I'm upfront about the process. These aren't hand-typed from scratch — they're directed, curated and polished. The concepts are mine. The typing is mostly not. And honestly? That's the whole point.
